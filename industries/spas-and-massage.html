What the code prevents in a spa

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not confirm a taken slot. It checks the calendar before offering: never two clients with the same therapist at the same time.

It does not invent a price. Package and membership prices come from your list. Anything missing is confirmed.

It does not book in the past. "This Saturday" is calculated in your spa's time zone, not the server's.

Frequently asked questions

Can it sell gift cards?

It explains them and how to buy them with what you tell it. Payment stays with your spa.

Does it understand the difference between my services?

The one you explain: upload your service menu and it answers from it. If something is not there, it does not make it up.

Can it answer tourists in other languages?

Yes: it replies in English, Spanish or Portuguese depending on the language the person writes in. Your services and prices stay as they are; only the reply is translated.

What if someone mentions a health condition?

It does not give health advice. If someone mentions an injury or a pregnancy, it answers with what you have set and, if needed, passes the conversation to the team.
