What the code prevents in a hair restoration or laser clinic

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not invent a price. An amount that is not in your information is removed. A transplant that depends on the consultation gets a consultation.

It does not confirm a taken slot. It checks the doctor's or the laser room's calendar before offering a time.

It does not book in the past. Dates are calculated in your clinic's time zone, "in six weeks" included.

Frequently asked questions

Can it assess a photo and say how many grafts are needed?

No. It describes what it sees so the conversation flows, but the assessment belongs to the doctor. What it does is book it.

Does it book laser sessions at the right interval?

It books each session on the requested date and sends a reminder beforehand. If the next session is booked when one ends, the interval is covered.

Is Hachi HIPAA compliant?

Hair restoration and laser removal are almost always paid out of pocket, and a clinic that never bills insurance is usually outside HIPAA. We still do not sign Business Associate Agreements today, so if any service you offer goes through insurance, raise it in the demo first.

Can it offer financing?

It explains the options you give it. Approval stays with the lender and your clinic.
