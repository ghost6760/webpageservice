What you lose today

Stopping treatment to grab the phone

Every interruption breaks the session. Not answering means a new patient who books another clinic that same afternoon.

The rescheduling shuffle

Plans of care run over many visits and life gets in the way. Moving one by hand, checking a colleague's schedule and confirming takes ten unbilled minutes.

Slots that stay empty

A late cancellation is a lost visit unless someone offers it to the patient who was waiting for an opening.

How Hachi answers

Kevin · Text · 1:21 pm

Hey, I have PT with Sarah Thursday at 5 but got stuck with a meeting, can I move it?

Sure, Kevin. With Sarah I have Thursday at 7:00 pm or Friday at 9:30 am. Does either work?

Friday 9:30

Changed: your session with Sarah is now Friday at 9:30 am and Thursday is free again. I'll text you a reminder the day before.

Nobody on the team stepped in

Moving an appointment is two steps: free the old one and create the new one. If the second fails, the first is rolled back; Kevin is never left with neither.

What the code prevents in a PT clinic

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not leave a reschedule half-done. If a step fails while moving a session, it is rolled back: the old one is never cancelled with the new one missing.

It does not confirm a taken slot. It checks that therapist's calendar before offering a time.

It does not double-book. Even if the patient sends the time in one message and the day in another.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hachi HIPAA compliant?

We do not sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement today. Practices that bill insurance are covered entities, so tell us in the demo before sharing patient data. Cash-pay practices that never bill insurance are usually not covered entities.

Does it know which therapist each patient sees?

It books with the provider the patient asks for or the one you set for that service. If someone says "with Sarah", it looks for a slot in Sarah's calendar.

What if someone asks whether their injury is serious?

It does not diagnose. It offers an evaluation with a therapist, and if the message sounds urgent, it hands it to a person.

Can it sell session packages?

It explains your packages and their prices and books the sessions. Payment stays with your clinic.
