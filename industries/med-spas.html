What the code prevents in a med spa

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not invent a price. An amount that is not in your price list, and that the client did not say, is removed before sending. In a med spa, a wrong price is an argument at the front desk.

It does not confirm a taken slot. It checks the injector's calendar before offering a time. If there is no slot, it offers another.

It does not double-book. Even if the client insists or sends the details across three messages, the consultation is created once.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hachi HIPAA compliant?

We do not sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement today, so we say it plainly. HIPAA applies when a practice bills insurance electronically; a med spa that is strictly cash-pay usually is not a covered entity. If you bill insurance for anything, tell us in the demo before sharing any patient data.

Can it explain a treatment without giving medical advice?

Yes, and that is how it is set up: it explains what you wrote about each treatment and books the consultation for the clinical decision. It does not diagnose or recommend doses.

Does it work with leads from my Instagram and Facebook ads?

Yes. On the Complete plan, when someone fills in your Meta lead form, Hachi contacts them within seconds and keeps the consent text they accepted.

What if a client wants to talk to a person?

The conversation goes to your team with all the context. Nobody gets stuck talking to a machine.
