What you lose today

The missed call is a new patient

People looking for a dentist call several practices. The one that does not answer loses a first visit that could have become an implant or an aligner case.

Recall appointments that slip

The six-month cleaning depends on someone remembering to call. When it slips, the chair sits empty and the patient comes back when it hurts.

No-shows in the chair

An empty chair for an hour is a hygienist's hour paid for and not billed. Most of the time a reminder and a quick "are you still coming?" would have prevented it.

What Hachi does for a dental practice

  • Answers texts, WhatsApp and, with voice, the practice phone: it books during the call.
  • Tells a cleaning from an exam or a new-patient visit, each with its own length and operatory or provider.
  • Recognises an emergency by what it means — pain, swelling, a broken tooth — not by single keywords, and hands it to your team.

  • Answers the repetitive questions with your information: hours, financing, which plans you accept, parking.

What the code prevents in a dental practice

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not lose an emergency. Severe pain or a request to speak to someone is escalated to a person, even if something fails internally.

It does not confirm a taken slot. It checks each operatory's calendar before offering a time. No free slot, no appointment.

It does not book in the past or the wrong time zone. Dates like "Monday the 21st" or "the day after tomorrow" are calculated by the code in your practice's time zone.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hachi HIPAA compliant?

Not today, and we would rather say so up front: we do not sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement yet. Most dental practices bill insurance and are covered entities, so tell us in the demo before sharing any patient data and we will tell you honestly whether it fits.

Can it quote prices for implants or aligners?

The ones you give it. If a treatment needs a quote after an exam, it says so and books the first visit. A figure that is not in your information is not sent.

What does it do with an after-hours emergency?

It flags it as urgent and hands it to your team straight away. What happens next — an on-call number, a first-thing appointment — is up to you and is configured that way.

Does it work with my practice management software?

Hachi has its own calendar with a copy in Google Calendar, and can send every appointment to another system via webhook. A specific integration is assessed in the demo.
