What you lose today

Halfway through a gel set

You cannot stop a manicure to answer the phone. And the client who texts wants to know now whether Friday is open.

Services that are not the same length

A gel polish, an acrylic fill or a lash set take very different times. A poorly planned book is an afternoon of running late.

The regular who forgets

If nobody reminds her the fill is due, it slips, a nail breaks and she ends up at the salon that did reply.

What the code prevents in a nail salon

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not confirm a taken slot. It only offers gaps where the whole service fits.

It does not invent a price. A custom design with no price on your list is confirmed at the salon, not improvised.

It does not double-book. Even if the client repeats the request in another message.

Frequently asked questions

Can it book a specific station?

Yes. Each station or tech is a calendar, and each service is assigned to the right one.

Does it remind clients when their next visit is due?

It sends a reminder for every booked appointment. If the next one is booked at checkout, the reminder goes out on its own.

Can I send promotions to my clients?

On the Complete plan, campaigns to your own client list, with the consent that list carries. Replies are handled by the assistant.

Does it understand nail-art photos?

Yes: it reads the image and replies accordingly. If the design needs to be quoted, it says so and offers a time.
