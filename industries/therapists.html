What the code prevents in a therapy practice

The model understands and converses; the critical rules are enforced by code (the guardrails), whatever the conversation says. The ones that matter most here:

It does not claim to be a person. If asked, it says it is an AI assistant. In a therapy practice, that honesty is not negotiable.

It does not lose a sensitive message. Anything that sounds urgent, or a request to speak to you, is escalated, even if something fails internally.

It does not mix data between practices. Each practice is isolated from the others; what one person writes cannot show up in another account.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hachi HIPAA compliant?

Not today: we do not sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement yet. If your practice bills insurance, you are likely a covered entity, so tell us in the demo before sharing client data and we will tell you honestly whether it fits.

What happens if someone writes in crisis?

The assistant does not try to handle it: it is set up to pass the message to you immediately and, if you choose, to share the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline or another number you pick. You decide that wording in advance.

Can it book telehealth sessions?

Yes: it is one more service with its own length. You set the video link.

Will clients notice it is an assistant?

It writes naturally and in your tone, but it does not hide what it is: if asked, it says so.
